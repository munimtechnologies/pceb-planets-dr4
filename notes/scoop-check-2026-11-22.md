# Scoop check, 2026-11-22 (before Gaia DR4)

Question: has anyone published, or posted, a test of the eclipse-timing companions of QS Vir, DE CVn,
RR Cae, HW Vir or V893 Sco against Gaia astrometry, or a prediction of what Gaia DR4 epoch astrometry
should show for them?

Searches (web, arXiv listings via search engines): "eclipse timing variations circumbinary planet
post-common envelope binary Gaia astrometry", "QS Vir / RR Cae / DE CVn Gaia astrometric acceleration
RUWE circumbinary", the arXiv pages of the papers below.

Found:
- Baycroft, Triaud & Kervella 2023 (arXiv:2309.05716): Hipparcos-Gaia proper-motion anomaly for HW Vir
  only (2 sigma, 214 +- 111 m/s); excludes four earlier HW Vir solutions; expects epoch astrometry to help.
- Baycroft, Triaud & Sahlmann 2026 (arXiv:2603.02198, MNRAS): population forecast; Table 4 gives Gaia
  **DR5** S/N for 32 PCEB candidates. No DR4 per-claim predictions, no epoch-level test, no grading rules.
- Pulley et al. 2022 (arXiv:2206.06919) and 2025 (arXiv:2507.06748): eclipse timing only; no Gaia.
- Gaia DR4 is not out (2026-12-02), so no DR4 test can exist yet.

Not found: any pre-registered per-claim DR4 prediction or test. This repository is, as far as we can
tell, the first.

Re-check right before publishing any result: arXiv astro-ph.SR/EP listings from 2026-12-02, Google
Scholar for each system name + "Gaia DR4", and DPAC's DR4 non-single-star tables for the six source_ids.
